Ravindra Pratap Singh Sisodia. Senior product designer in Bengaluru, five years across B2B SaaS, fintech and enterprise. Designs in Figma, builds in Claude Code, ships real Mac apps.

his eyeline

01 / Aperture

The same silhouette, three different things behind it.

Profile traced from the photograph above

Behind the silhouette, one

Jodhpur, where the eye was trained.

Blue houses against red sandstone under hard desert light. Nothing in that city is subtle about colour, and nothing about it is accidental either. Flat colour, hard shadow, clear edges. That is the whole grammar.

Behind the silhouette, two

The road out, where it gets quiet.

A Royal Enfield parked on a dirt track through tea country, helmet resting on the bar, rider somewhere out of frame. Pixels by day, petrol by dusk. The only thing that reliably empties his head.

Behind the silhouette, three

What actually gets shipped.

Crew: ten pixel species, seven Claude Code hooks, no server and no account. Free for macOS. The design and the binary are the same job now, which is the part most portfolios still separate.

03 / Contours

The tea rows were already drawing the hill.

Every row on that slope follows one elevation, which makes the whole plantation a contour map somebody planted by hand. Trace them and you get a line system, and the line system is where this page gets its dividers.

Six lines traced off the terraces. The amber one is the track itself.

04 / Registration

Three plates, slightly out of register, pulled into line.

A press sheet is wrong until every plate lands on the same spot. So is a build. Scroll and the three inks converge, which is the shortest honest way to describe the distance between a high fidelity file and the thing that actually ships.
