02 / Crew

macOS, 2026

A tiny crew of pixel monsters for your Claude Code agents.

One creature per chat and subagent, living along the bottom of your screen. A glance tells you which agent is working, which is stuck waiting on you, and which finished ten minutes ago.

Why it exists

You can run four or five Claude Code sessions at once and all of it is invisible. Reading a dashboard costs a glance, a parse and a context switch. So the state is a creature instead: you notice something is wrong from the corner of your eye, before you have consciously looked at anything.

The rule it is built on

It never invents a state. Every animation is driven by a real hook event.
